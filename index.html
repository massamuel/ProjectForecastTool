Forecast assumptions

Uses one blended labor cost rate. Excludes expenses, revenue, scheduling, and staffing constraints. Scope deltas represent remaining work; already spent hours remain in final cost. The effort ratio is capped between 0.5× and 3×. This MVP uses transparent rules, not trained ML.
